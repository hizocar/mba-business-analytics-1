## One company, six sessions

The whole module follows **Altamar Hotels**, a Chilean hotel group with three properties: a business hotel in **Santiago**, a beach hotel in **Viña del Mar**, and a lake hotel in **Puerto Varas**. Its management team has two questions:

<div class="hz-sections" markdown>

<div class="hz-section-card" markdown>
<p class="hz-kicker">Revenue management</p>
### What rate can we charge?
How much should a room-night cost, given the hotel, the season, the room, and how far ahead the guest books? A pricing question, answered with **regression**.
</div>

<div class="hz-section-card" markdown>
<p class="hz-kicker">Operations</p>
### Which bookings will cancel?
One booking in three is cancelled. Which ones, and how many rooms can we safely oversell? A risk question, answered with **classification**.
</div>

</div>

Straight lines answer part of both questions. Seasons that differ by hotel, early-bird discounts, last-minute premiums, and bookings made months ahead do not follow straight lines, so the module ends with the models that capture them: **decision trees, random forests, and gradient boosting**.

## How it works

<div class="hz-steps" markdown>

<div markdown>
<span class="hz-step">01</span>
**A live session each week (4 hours).** Concepts explained with interactive simulators, then the same idea applied to Altamar's data with ready-to-run code. The last part of each session is a clinic for your applied case.
</div>

<div markdown>
<span class="hz-step">02</span>
**A self-paced lab each week (2 hours).** A notebook that runs as it is: you change the settings, run it, and interpret what comes out. It ends with a short graded quiz.
</div>

<div markdown>
<span class="hz-step">03</span>
**No programming from scratch.** The code is written for you and explained line by line. Your job is to understand what it does, adjust it, and decide what the results mean for the business.
</div>

<div markdown>
<span class="hz-step">04</span>
**An applied case in teams.** You use the course toolkit on a business dataset and present recommendations to a management audience. It is 70% of the grade; the six quizzes are the other 30%.
</div>

</div>
